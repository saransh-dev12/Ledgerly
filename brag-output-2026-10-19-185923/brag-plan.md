# Ledgerly — launch film plan (`brag-slim`)

## Product and angle

Ledgerly is a personal finance tracker for people who want a clearer view of income, spending, and their remaining balance. Its strongest distinction in this build is a calm, black-and-lime interface paired with a direct “Give every rupee a direction.” promise. The film follows one real use path: arrive on the landing page, open the dashboard, record a small expense, and find it in the filtered activity list.

- **Audience:** people tracking everyday personal spending.
- **Hook:** the product's actual landing headline, revealed with a quiet lime focus sweep.
- **Visual identity:** near-black `#050505`, warm-white `#f2f2ed`, lime `#bdf27b`, Manrope and DM Mono as used by the app.
- **Tone:** polished fintech; cinematic wipes and camera-like scale changes, restrained light, clear UI, no stock finance graphics or unsupported claims.
- **Demo data:** seeded sample totals from Ledgerly plus a synthetic Coffee / ₹240 entry entered through the actual Add transaction form in an isolated local preview.

## Storyboard (20 seconds, landscape)

1. **0–4s — Give every rupee a direction.** The actual landing UI settles into view; the headline gets a measured focus reveal.
2. **4–7s — See the whole picture.** A lime-to-black wipe reveals the real dashboard and its seeded balance, income, expense, and savings summaries.
3. **7–11s — Record the moment.** The real Add transaction dialog opens. “Coffee” and “240” are entered and saved through the form, updating the totals.
4. **11–17s — Follow the flow.** The real Money out control filters activity and scrolls to the table, where the new Coffee row is visible.
5. **17–20s — Brand close.** The interface recedes behind a dark veil; Ledgerly and the real tagline resolve with the music credit on screen.
